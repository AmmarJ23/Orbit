The brief

Orbit is a local desktop task companion: Redmine and ClickUp tasks become low-poly planets circling on source orbits, and finished work falls into a separate system. The mark should keep the current icon's faceted planet and comet-blue ring, but read as a low-poly world rather than a cube, and hold up at 16 px.

calmfocusedspatialcrafted

Keep the hex planet + ring equity; make it unmistakably low-poly; work in one colour, on midnight and on white, and at favicon size.

Shaded Facets

The app's own low-poly planet: ten lit facets inside the familiar hexagon, with one comet-blue orbit passing in front.

  • Keeps the current icon's hexagon, ring and blues, so it still reads as Orbit
  • An icosphere cut with no centre 'Y', so it reads as a planet, not a cube
  • The five facet tones are the app palette; the planets in the scene share the mark's DNA
  • Small-size cut merges facets into three tones; the one-colour version keeps the facets as seams
