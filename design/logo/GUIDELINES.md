# Orbit logo: usage guide

**Mark:** Shaded Facets. The app's low-poly planet: a hexagon cut into ten lit facets (an icosphere with no cube-like centre "Y"), with one comet-blue orbit passing in front.
**Wordmark:** custom geometric lowercase `orbit`, drawn from rings and bars. The tittle on the `i` is a small hex planet. It contains no font, so no font licence applies.

Masters are generated by [`build.py`](build.py) (requires `shapely`): `python build.py masters`.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep a gap equal to **half the planet's width** (40 u on the 256 grid) on every side of the symbol or lockup.
- **Minimum sizes:**
  - Full symbol: 40 px.
  - From 32 px down to 16 px: use the small-size cut.
  - Horizontal lockup: 120 px wide.
  - Stacked lockup: 64 px wide.
  - Wordmark alone: 72 px wide.
- **Reversed:** on dark backgrounds use the dark set. Do not invert the light set.

## Backgrounds

- **Approved:** white and cool white `#F3F6FC` (light set); midnight `#0C1222` and deep navy `#151E32` (dark set).
- **On photos or busy space art:** use the app icon tile, or the one-colour white mark over a dark, calm area.

## Don't

- Recolour individual facets, add gradients, glows or bevels, or re-light the planet from a different side.
- Remove the gap between the ring and the planet, or move the ring behind the planet.
- Rotate, skew or stretch the mark, or change the ring's tilt (−24°).
- Re-set the wordmark in a typeface. Use the outlined masters.
- Use the 10-facet symbol below 40 px. The facets turn to noise; switch to the small-size cut.
- Put the light set on dark backgrounds, or the dark set on white.

## Handover notes

- **Rationale:** this is an evolution of the original icon. It keeps the hexagon, ring and blue palette, drops the second ring and the moon, and replaces the six-wedge "cube" shading with a ten-facet icosphere cut so the planet reads as low-poly.
- **Tests run:**
  - Rendered at 16, 24, 32, 64 and 96 px.
  - One-colour and reversed versions.
  - App-icon and favicon contexts.
  - Audit (`svg_audit.py`): no live text, rasters, filters or masks. The audit's near-miss-angle warnings come from the ring's curve being stored as short polyline segments, which is expected.
- **Open items:**
  - The masters store curves as dense polylines. A vector editor pass could redraw the ring as true elliptical arcs to make the files smaller.
  - The app icon is applied in `desktop/src-tauri/icons/`. `icon.icns` and `icon.ico` use the small-size cut at 16 and 32 px (and at 48 px in the `.ico`) and the full symbol from 64 px up.
  - A trademark search is recommended before any public release. Clearance cannot be guaranteed from here.
